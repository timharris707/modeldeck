# ModelDeck 1.1.26

A new setting, `providerUsageReadsEnabled`, lets a Mac take its usage numbers only from what the provider programs report. When it is off, ModelDeck makes no usage call with a stored sign-in and renews none, either on its schedule or when you press Refresh. It is on by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it off.

On a Mac that reads another Mac's usage page, a card whose newest reading came from the page now says how old it is and where it came from, for example "Read 10 min ago · M5", instead of "Uses the pool on …".

Rollback floor: unchanged
