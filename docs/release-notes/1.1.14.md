# ModelDeck 1.1.14

The second half of "updating should feel smoother": the background service restarts in place after an update, the update dialog and the menu bar tell you where things stand, and notifications do something when you click them.

**Updates no longer re-register the background service.** After every update the app used to unregister and re-register the service with macOS, wait up to nearly a minute before showing any data, show a "Background service updated" banner every time, and sometimes drop you into "Waiting for your approval" in Login Items. Now an ordinary update restarts the service in place, the deck reads data from the old service immediately while the restart happens alongside, and the next refresh shows the new build. No banner, and Login Items approval is never touched on that path. The full re-register still exists as a fallback, used only when the restart does not take or the service definition itself changed. Worst-case wait dropped from about 55 seconds to about 15. (#678)

**One feed decides whether an update exists.** The app's update check now reads the same feed the installer uses, so the two can never disagree. Release notes ride that feed, so the update dialog shows them even for releases published from another machine. (#685)

**The menu-bar icon shows a staged update.** While an update is downloaded and waiting for a restart, the glyph carries one small dot at its top-right. It never uses or competes with the health colours. (#685)

**Notifications are clickable.** Clicking "ModelDeck x.y.z is ready" restarts to finish the update, the same as the deck banner's Restart. Clicking a usage warning opens the deck. (#685)

**Also**
- An update the feed says needs a newer macOS than yours is skipped instead of offered and then failing to install. (#685)
