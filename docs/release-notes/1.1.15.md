# ModelDeck 1.1.15

A small release for everyone about to upgrade to macOS 27.

**The deck no longer gets stuck on "Background service starting…" after a reboot.** When your Mac starts up, the app and the background service start at the same moment. If the service takes a while to open its database before it begins answering (a large database, a cold disk, the first boot after an OS upgrade), the app used to check once, get no answer, and sit on the "starting" card until you clicked Check Again, even though your data was already showing underneath. The app now keeps checking for up to a minute and clears the card on its own the moment the service answers. If the service really is not coming up, the card and Check Again stay exactly as before. (#697)

**Also**
- Clicking Check Again or Retry while a service repair is already in progress no longer starts a second, overlapping repair. (#688)
