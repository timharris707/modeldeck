# ModelDeck 1.1.23

Macs that use another Mac's proxy pool show the pool's routing weights and its sign-in warnings on their own deck again. They read them from the pool Mac's usage page every minute, matched by account rather than by name. If the page stops answering or its numbers are 10 minutes old, the badges disappear rather than show an old number.

Accounts on Anthropic's Ultra plan now say "Ultra" on the deck as soon as Claude saves the new plan name.

After ModelDeck has been used on 10 different days, the deck may once show a dismissible line asking for a GitHub star. Starring or dismissing it ends it for good.
