# ModelDeck 1.1.24

A Mac that uses another Mac's proxy pool no longer asks you to sign in to each account on it. Its cards say they use the pool instead, and warn when the pool's page stops answering.

Such a Mac can now also show numbers from the usage page it reads. Its Claude cards take each account's latest reading from the page, matched by account rather than by name, and say how old the reading is and where it came from, for example "Read 9 min ago · Linux · T3". A reading this Mac took itself still wins when it is newer. If the page stops answering or its readings are 10 minutes old, the cards go back to this Mac's own numbers.

Rollback floor: unchanged
